`Badge`, `StatusChip`, `Chip`, `Avatar`, `Stat`, `Progress`, `Spinner`, `Switch`, `Select`, `Sheet`, `Dialog`, `Alert`, `Divider`, `Kbd`, `EmptyState` and `Skeleton` forward the attributes they do not own (`data-testid`, `id`, `title`, `aria-*`, handlers) and type them, so `<Badge tone="success" data-testid="paid">` passes `svelte-check` and reaches the DOM (#1066). They land on the element that represents the component: the pill or the anchored badge's wrapper, the `<dialog>` of `Dialog` and `Sheet`, the native `<input role="switch">` of `Switch` and the trigger `<button>` of `Select` (whose `class` stays on the wrapper, like `TextField` and `Checkbox`). `class` and `style` merge with the component's own. A live-region default (`role="status"` of `StatusChip`, `EmptyState` and `Spinner`, `role="alert"` of `Alert`) gives way to a caller's `role`; structural semantics (`progressbar`, `separator`, `switch`, `dialog`, the select's popup state) stay owned. `Switch` and `Select` use a caller's `id` for the control and its label, and `Select`, `Dialog` and `Sheet` add a caller's `aria-describedby` to their own. `Sheet` gains `class`.
