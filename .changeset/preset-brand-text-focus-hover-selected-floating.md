Preset roles `brandText`, `focusRing`, `hover`, `selected` and `floating` (`SchemeColors`): the brand text colour, the focus ring, the hover and selected fills (translucent allowed) and a fourth surface step above `overlay` (`--ca-surface-floating`, read by Popover, DropdownMenu, Select, Combobox, Dialog and Toast; unset keeps `overlay`). `presetContrastIssues` measures `brandText` at 4.5:1, `focusRing` at 3:1 and the text roles over the hover and selected fills.
