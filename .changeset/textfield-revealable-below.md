`TextField`: `revealable` adds a show/hide eye button to `type="password"` (`aria-pressed`, localized name, 44px hit area on touch, no layout shift), `below` renders a snippet under the field and its hint or error (a "Forgot password?" link, a strength meter) and `requiredIndicator={false}` hides the asterisk of a `required` field. New i18n group `textField` (en, pt-BR) (#348).
