New `Toggle`: a single pressed/unpressed button (`aria-pressed`, `bind:pressed`, `size`, `variant="outlined" | "ghost"`, `label` or `icon` with `aria-label`) that looks exactly like a `ToggleGroup` item. Icon-only `ToggleGroup` items now also reach the touch target in width under a coarse pointer.
