PageShell: `icon` (decorative tile before the title, `--ca-page-icon-size`, `--ca-page-icon-bg`), `count` (a badge after the title, part of the heading's and the region's accessible name) and a `titleExtra` snippet. KpiCard: `iconStyle="tile"` with `tone` (`brand`, `success`, `warning`, `error`) puts the icon in a tinted tile above the label (`--ca-kpi-tile-size`, `--ca-kpi-tile-bg`, `--ca-kpi-tile-fg`); the default corner icon is unchanged.
