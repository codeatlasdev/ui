Field variants: `variant?: "outlined" | "filled" | "underline"` on TextField, Select, Combobox, NumberField, ColorField and the date fields, and `components.field.variant` for the whole theme (`data-ca-field`). `components.field` also takes `height`, `radius` and `borderWidth`. Forced colors keep an edge. The Theme Studio randomizer now varies the field variant, border width, button weight and tracking and draws from all nine surfaces, and the Form preview shows a Switch. Unset, fields look as before.
