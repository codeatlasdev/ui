`ButtonGroup` (#448, part of #432): attaches `Button`, `IconButton` and a `DropdownMenu` trigger into one `role="group"` control, horizontal or vertical (`orientation`), with square inner corners, a single-line seam and the hovered or keyboard-focused member raised over it. Replaces the shadcn Button Group row in the React equivalents table.
