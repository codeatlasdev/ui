`PreviewStage`: a resizable device frame for showcasing a component or a whole page at 390, 768, 1280 or any width. A segmented width control and a keyboard-operable splitter set the frame width; a frame wider than the stage is scaled whole. Inline content is themed in its own `ThemeIsland` (`scheme`, `preset`); `src` shows a same-origin page in an isolated iframe.
