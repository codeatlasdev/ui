Stat: `labelPlacement` (`above`/`below`), `labelCase` (`upper`/`sentence`) and `valueFont` (`display`/`sans`), plus the `--ca-stat-label-size`, `--ca-stat-label-weight`, `--ca-stat-value-font`, `--ca-stat-value-weight`, `--ca-stat-value-line-height` and `--ca-stat-gap` tokens. Defaults are unchanged.
