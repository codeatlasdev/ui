AudioPlayer: `tone="inherit"` draws bars, chips and time from the surrounding text colour so it reads on a brand-filled `MessageBubble`; `variant="voice"` gives a round filled play button; the sizing tokens `--ca-audio-bar-w`, `--ca-audio-bar-gap`, `--ca-audio-wave-h`, `--ca-audio-max-w` and `--ca-audio-min-w` are public and the bar count follows their computed values. `MessageBubble` sets `--ca-audio-surface`.
