`Badge` renders an inline status pill: `<Badge tone="danger" subtle dot size="md">Overdue</Badge>` (tones `brand`, `success`, `danger`, `warning`, `info`, `neutral`; `subtle` is a tinted background whose label clears 4.5:1 in light and dark). `Button` gains `trailingHint` (a `Kbd` in `trailing` is hidden below `md` and on touch with no reserved gap) and `align="between"` (label at the start, `trailing` at the end; the spinner keeps its place). Defaults are unchanged.
