`AspectRatio` (#446, part of #432): a box that holds `ratio` for a video, an embed or any child with no size of its own; a direct `img`, `video`, `iframe`, `canvas` or `svg` child fills it. `Image` gains `ratio` (crop to a width / height box, filling the container) and `fit` (`cover` or `contain`). Replaces the Radix `AspectRatio` row of the React equivalence table.
