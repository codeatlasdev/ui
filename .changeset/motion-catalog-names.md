Demo sidecars accept `names` (motion vocabulary) and `motion` (exact property, duration, easing, delay, stagger, spring). They are printed in `e/NNN.txt` and `p/NNN.txt`, carried in `catalog.json` and `catalog.tsv`, and `ca-ui-mcp` `search_demos` matches names and aliases before summary and tags.
