New optional entry `@codeatlasdev/ui/sound`: opt-in, synthesized interface sound with no audio files (under 4 KB gzip). One shared `AudioContext` is created only inside the first pointer or key event after the user turns sound on with `<SoundToggle />` (`aria-pressed`, accessible name, optional volume slider); the choice persists in `localStorage` key `ca-sound` and sound is off by default. Every cue is a short oscillator and noise recipe behind four profiles (`system`, `glass`, `brutal`, `terminal`) that follow the design style through `--ca-sound-profile` (written by `designStyle()`), tunable with `--ca-sound-gain`, `--ca-sound-pitch` and `--ca-sound-brightness`. Hover and slider tick cues are off under `prefers-reduced-motion`; sound is suspended while the tab is hidden or media plays, with one cue per name per 40 ms and at most four voices. Button, Switch, Toast (`toast.*(msg, { sound })`), Dialog, Tabs and Slider take a `sound` prop (`boolean | CueName`) that emits through `soundBus`, a no-op hook exported from the root, so core stays silent without the entry. `use:sound` (the `sound` export is also the action) plays a cue on any DOM event. Docs: Feedback > Sound, and the Sound principles guide.
