Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #219 "Table row sparklines" (charts/sparklines).
What it shows: A table of four products with an inline sparkline per row and a text trend label. When to use: Adding a mini trend line to table rows, such as revenue history per product.
Spec:
- `<Card material="glass" flush full>` wraps a plain `<table>`
- `rows` has 4 products: Atlas Pro, Nova Suite, Relay SDK, Mesh API, each with 6 `revenue` values and a `trend`
- `<Sparkline data={row.revenue} trend={row.trend} height="32px" />` in each row
- `labels` maps `up`, `down` and `neutral` to `'▲ Growing'`, `'▼ Declining'` and `'→ Stable'`
- Columns: Product, Last 6 months, Trend
