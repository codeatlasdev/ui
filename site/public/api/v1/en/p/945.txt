Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #945 "Bar open state" (navigation/menubar).
What it shows: A two-menu bar (Share, Insert) with a line reporting whether any menu is open through onOpenChange. When to use: Reacting to the bar as a whole, for example dimming a canvas while a menu is open, without tracking each menu.
Spec:
- `<Menubar aria-label="Document" onOpenChange={(o) => (open = o)}>` with two `MenubarMenu`s, Share and Insert
- `onOpenChange` fires when the bar goes from none open to one open and back, not when the pointer or arrows switch between menus
- A line reads "A menu is" followed by open or closed
