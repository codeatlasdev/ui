Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #842 "Your own rule" (inputs/password-strength).
What it shows: A strength meter driven by your own length rule, with custom label text. When to use: Apps whose password policy is length-based or comes from a server rule.
Spec:
- `const byLength = (v: string) => (v.length === 0 ? 0 : v.length >= 12 ? 3 : v.length >= 8 ? 2 : 1)`
- `value={password}` and `score={byLength}` on `<PasswordStrength`
- `labels={{ empty: 'Type a passphrase', weak: 'Too short', fair: 'Almost there', strong: 'Good length' }}`
- Field is `<TextField label="Passphrase" type="password" bind:value={password} />`
