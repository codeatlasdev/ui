Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #942 "File menu" (navigation/context-menu).
What it shows: A dashed file area that opens a Rename, Pinned, Move to and Delete menu at the pointer on right-click, at the finger on a long touch press, or from the keyboard with Shift+F10. When to use: Context menus on rows, files or canvases that reuse the DropdownMenu items, including checkbox items and submenus, without wiring any event handler.
Spec:
- `<ContextMenu class="area" role="group" aria-label="Project file">` wraps the region content and takes the items in a `{#snippet menu()}`
- The menu holds `DropdownMenuItem`, a `<DropdownMenuCheckboxItem bind:checked={pinned}>`, a `DropdownMenuSub` with `DropdownMenuSubTrigger`, a `DropdownMenuSeparator` and a destructive Delete
- Each action item calls `function pick(action: string)`, which sets `last` and calls `announce`
- Pinned (yes or no) and the last action are shown below the area
