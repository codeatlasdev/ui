Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #610 "Map, split and page" (layout/page-frame).
What it shows: A PageFrame floating a 352px panel over a stand-in map, with a mode switch (map, split, page), a side switch and a live readout of the covered pixels from stageInsets. When to use: Map-first screens where a panel sits over a live stage and the stage must keep pins and routes clear of what the panel covers.
Spec:
- A 380px tall frame holds a map stand-in and `<PageFrame mode={mode as 'map' | 'split' | 'page'} side={side as 'left' | 'right'} width="sm" label="Territory" headerBorder>`
- `{#snippet header()}` has an h2 Territory and a line reading the covered px from `stageInsets.current`
- `{#snippet toolbar()}` has a `TextField` labelled Search
- Body holds two `SegmentedControl`s: Mode (Map, Split, Page) and Side (Left, Right)
- `{#snippet footer()}` has a filled small `Button` Save
- A pin on the map shifts with the covered area
