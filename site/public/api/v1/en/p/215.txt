Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #215 "Clustered by tier" (charts/scatter).
What it shows: A scatter plot with three named series, Premium, Standard and Free, of 30 points each. When to use: Showing segments or clusters of customers as separate colour-coded groups.
Spec:
- `const series: ScatterSeries[]` with 3 entries: `'Premium'`, `'Standard'`, `'Free'`
- Each series uses `cluster(30,` to generate 30 `[number, number]` points
- `color` is optional on a series, omitted here so the palette assigns one per series
- `<ScatterChart {series} yName="Conversion %"` with `yFormatter={(v) => `${v.toFixed(1)}%`}`
- `height="340px"` inside `<Card material="glass" full>`
