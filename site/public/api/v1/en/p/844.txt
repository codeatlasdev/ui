Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #844 "Boot" (feedback/splash-screen).
What it shows: A button that boots a fake app: the splash shows at least 800ms, waits for ready, then leaves and reports ondone. When to use: App shells that load a session or data before the first screen and must not flash the splash.
Spec:
- `<SplashScreen {ready} minDuration={800} ondone={() => seen++}>` with a `logo` snippet holding `<strong class="mark">Acme</strong>`
- `start()` sets `ready = false`, bumps `boot` and sets `ready = true` after `1200` ms
- Wrapped in `{#key boot}` and rendered only once `boot > 0`
- A caption shows `Splash screens shown: {seen}`
