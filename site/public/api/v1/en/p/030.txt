Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #030 "Basic" (inputs/number-field).
What it shows: Two NumberField inputs in a Stack: an integer quantity with bounds and a decimal price with a step. When to use: Quantity and price steppers in order or cart forms.
Spec:
- `<Stack gap="4">` wraps both fields
- `<NumberField label="Quantity" bind:value={qty} min={0} max={99} />` with `let qty = $state(1)`
- `<NumberField label="Price" bind:value={price} min={0} step={0.01} />` with `let price = $state(9.99)`
