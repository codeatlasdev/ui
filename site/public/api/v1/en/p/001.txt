Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #001 "Full width" (inputs/button).
What it shows: Two buttons that stretch to the width of their container: a filled primary and an outlined cancel. When to use: Stacked action pairs in forms, dialogs and bottom sheets where the buttons should span the column.
Spec:
- `fullWidth` on `Button` makes it fill its parent
- Primary action keeps the default variant, secondary uses `variant="outlined"`
- Wrapper column is `max-width: 320px` with `gap: var(--ca-space-2)`
