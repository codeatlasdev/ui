Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #781 "Full page" (layout/theme-island).
What it shows: Full-page ThemeIsland with `document`: html and body take the island's preset and scheme while it is mounted. When to use: A public landing or auth page with its own preset and scheme inside an app that uses another one.
Spec:
- `<ThemeIsland preset="rose" scheme="light" document>` is the page root
- Closing it restores the app preset and scheme
