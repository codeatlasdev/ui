Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #996 "Scrolling filter row" (display/chip).
What it shows: A row of eight filter chips that scrolls sideways inside a narrow frame, with a fade where more chips are hidden. When to use: A filter or message-type row on a phone that must scroll without clipping the 44px touch area of its chips.
Spec:
- `<ChipRow label="Filter chats" fade>` wraps the chips in one scrolling group
- `const filters = ['All', 'Unread', 'Groups', 'Archived', 'Starred', 'Muted', 'Mentions', 'Drafts']` lists the eight filters
- `<Chip label={f} selected={active === f} onclick={() => (active = f)} />` makes each one a toggle
