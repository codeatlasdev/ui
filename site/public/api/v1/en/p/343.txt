Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #343 "Like burst" (motion/micro).
What it shows: A like button whose heart pops to 1.45 and throws eight particles outward while the count ticks up; aria-pressed carries the state. When to use: Like, favorite or reaction buttons where a positive action deserves a small celebration.
Spec:
- `MotionStage` from `@codeatlasdev/ui` with `loop={2600}`
- `aria-pressed={liked}` on the button, count in `tabular-nums`
- `const PARTICLES = 8;` each `<i style="--a:{i * (360 / PARTICLES)}deg">` is one dot on a ring
- `@keyframes like-pop` scales the heart `1` to `1.45` and back
- `@keyframes like-particle` moves each dot `translateY(-26px)` while it fades to `opacity: 0` and `scale(0.3)`
- `{#key burst}` remounts the ring so the burst replays on every like; nothing bursts when reduced
Motion:
- transform: scale (heart): 460ms cubic-bezier(0.175, 0.885, 0.32, 1.275)
- transform, opacity (particles): 560ms cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.2, 1)
