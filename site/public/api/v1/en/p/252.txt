Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #252 "Voice messages" (display/audio-player).
What it shows: Two voice message players: one with a waveform from peaks, one with flat placeholder bars. When to use: Voice notes in chat where the audio loads only on the first play and can be scrubbed.
Spec:
- `<AudioPlayer {src} {peaks} duration={2} />`
- `<AudioPlayer {src} duration={2} label="Without waveform data" />`
- `const peaks = Array.from({ length: 48 }` builds the waveform amplitudes
- `tone(2)` builds a 2 second WAV blob as the source
