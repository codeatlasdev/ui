Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #323 "Reading progress bar" (motion/scroll).
What it shows: A 4px bar stuck to the top of an article fills from left to right as it is read, with a percentage readout, tied directly to scroll position. When to use: Long articles, docs and legal pages where the reader wants to know how far they are.
Spec:
- `MotionStage` from `@codeatlasdev/ui` with `loop={5200}`: autoplay scrolls the article, a wheel or touch scroll takes over
- `.doc__bar` is `position: sticky;` with `block-size: 4px;` and holds a fill scaled with `transform: scaleX(var(--p));` from `transform-origin: left center;`
- Native: `animation-timeline: scroll(nearest block)` with `@keyframes fill` from `scaleX(0)` to `scaleX(1)`, inside `@supports (animation-timeline: scroll())`
- Fallback and readout: a `scroll` listener computes `el.scrollTop / max` (`max` is `el.scrollHeight - el.clientHeight`); the percentage is `Math.round(p * 100)`
- Autoplay reads the article in `3600` ms, linear like a steady reader
- Under reduced motion the bar is full and the readout shows 100%
Motion:
- transform: scaleX: animation-timeline: scroll(nearest block) linear
