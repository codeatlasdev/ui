Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #052 "Settings list" (inputs/switch).
What it shows: Settings list of three rows, each with a title, a subtitle and a Switch. When to use: Account preferences screen with one toggle per setting.
Spec:
- 3 rows in `.settings-list`: Email notifications, Dark mode, Usage analytics

- Each switch has an `aria-label` matching its row title, e.g. `aria-label="Dark mode"`
- Initial state: email `$state(true)`, dark and analytics `$state(false)`
