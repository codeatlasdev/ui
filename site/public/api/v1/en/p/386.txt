Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #386 "Card tilt with glare" (motion/hero-ambient).
What it shows: A payment card that leans up to 10 degrees toward the pointer on a spring while a soft glare slides across its surface. When to use: Product cards, pricing tiers, credit cards and badges that should feel like physical objects on hover.
Spec:
- `const MAX = 10`: the card leans at most 10 degrees (`ry.target = nx * MAX`, `rx.target = -ny * MAX`)
- Two springs: `new Spring(0, { stiffness: 0.12, damping: 0.55 })` for `rx` and for `ry`
- `transform: rotateX(var(--rx)) rotateY(var(--ry))` inside a `perspective: 700px` parent; the glare is `radial-gradient(circle at var(--gx) var(--gy), rgb(255 255 255 / 0.38), transparent 55%)`
- Disabled on touch: `matchMedia('(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)')` sets `fine`, and `@media (hover: none), (pointer: coarse)` removes the transform
- Autoplay sweeps `sweep` every `loop={1500}`; under reduced motion the card is flat (`--rx:{m.reduced || !fine ? 0 : rx.current}deg`)
Motion:
- transform: rotateX, rotateY; glare position: spring spring spring { stiffness: 0.12, damping: 0.55 }
