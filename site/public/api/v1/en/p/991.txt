Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #991 "Text color" (inputs/color-field).
What it shows: Color field for a text color measured against a white page, with a sample line in that color. When to use: Choosing text colors that must reach WCAG AA.
Spec:
- `<ColorField` with `label="Body text"`, `bind:value={text}`, `against={page}`, `minRatio={4.5}` and `format="oklch"`
- `page` is the constant `'#ffffff'`
- Initial value is `$state('#6b7280')`
- Below the field, a white box shows a sample sentence painted in the current color
