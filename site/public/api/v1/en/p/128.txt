What it shows: An Image with a two-rendition `srcset` (400w and 800w) and a `sizes` hint, in a 420px wrapper. When to use: Responsive content images where the browser should pick the smaller file on narrow screens.
Spec:

- `sizes="(min-width: 768px) 420px, 100vw"`
- `src={large}` with `width={800}` and `height={450}`
- `alt="Two renditions of the same 16 by 9 picture"`
- Wrapper is `<div style="max-width: 420px">`
