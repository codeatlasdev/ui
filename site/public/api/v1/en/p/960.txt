Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #960 "ratio and fit" (display/image).
What it shows: One 4:3 gradient file rendered in three boxes: ratio 16/9 (cropped), ratio 1 (cropped) and ratio 1 with fit contain (letterboxed). When to use: Card covers, thumbnails and avatar grids where every picture must share one box whatever its own proportions.
Spec:
- `<Image {src} alt="Wide cover" width={800} height={600} ratio={16 / 9} />`
- `<Image {src} alt="Square cover" width={800} height={600} ratio={1} />`
- `<Image {src} alt="Contained" width={800} height={600} ratio={1} fit="contain" />`
- `src` is an inlined 4:3 `data:image/svg+xml;utf8,` gradient with a circle
- Each image sits in a `<figure>` with a `<figcaption>` naming its ratio
