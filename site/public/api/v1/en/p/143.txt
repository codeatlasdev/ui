Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #143 "Custom delay" (display/tooltip).
What it shows: Three tooltips with different open delays on glass buttons: instant, default and slow. When to use: Tuning how quickly a tooltip appears, instant for dense toolbars and slower for hints that should not flash.
Spec:
- `delay={0}` on the Instant button with `content="No delay (delay=0)"`
- No `delay` prop on the Default button, `content="Default delay (500ms)"`
- `delay={1000}` on the Slow button
- Each trigger uses `{#snippet triggerChild(props)}` with `<Button variant="glass" {...props}>`
