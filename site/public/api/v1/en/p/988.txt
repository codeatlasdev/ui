Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #988 "Hear it" (feedback/sound).
What it shows: A sound toggle with volume, a profile picker (system, glass, brutalism, terminal) and a board of buttons that each play one cue. When to use: Let people audition a sound profile before turning interface sound on in an app.
Spec:
- `<SoundToggle volume />` next to a `SegmentedControl` bound to the profile
- `$effect(() => sound.use(PROFILES[profile]))` pins the picked profile and `onDestroy` releases it
- A `role="group"` board of outlined small `Button` items, each with `sound={cue}` for one cue name
- The cues are `press`, `toggle-on`, `toggle-off`, `success`, `error`, `warning`, `open`, `close` and `tab`
