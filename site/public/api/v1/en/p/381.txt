Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #381 "Grid ripple" (motion/hero-ambient).
What it shows: A grid of dots where a click sends a wave outward: each dot swells and fades back after a delay equal to its distance from the click. When to use: Empty states, hero backdrops and interactive posters where a click or tap should feel like it disturbs a surface.
Spec:
- `MotionStage` with `loop={2200}`; each cycle starts a ripple from the next entry of `origins`
- The delay is the distance in cells times 70: `--d:{Math.round(dist * 70)}ms`, passed to `animation: rip-wave 700ms cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) var(--d) both`
- `rip-wave` keyframes: `transform: scale(2.6)` at the peak, then back to `scale(1)`, colored with `var(--ca-brand-fill)` at the peak
- `{#key src.n}` remounts the dots on every click so all delays restart from the new origin
- Under reduced motion `class:rip__dot--run={!m.reduced}` drops the animation and every dot stays at rest
Motion:
- transform: scale, background-color: 700ms cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) stagger 70ms
Names: Grid ripple | Dot ripple | Click ripple | Ripple from point | Ondulação em grade | Onda de pontos a partir do clique | Ondas en cuadrícula
