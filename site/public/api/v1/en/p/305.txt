Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #305 "Shine sweep" (motion/text).
What it shows: A heading painted with a brand gradient and a light band that sweeps across it in 2200ms. Once you move the pointer or press arrow keys the band follows you. When to use: Hero and pricing headlines, a highlighted keyword, a premium badge or a loading title that needs a single glint of attention.
Spec:
- `MotionStage` from `@codeatlasdev/ui` with `label="Shine sweep: a highlight glides across a gradient heading"` and `loop={3200}`
- `background-size: 250% 100%` with `background-clip: text` makes the gradient show through the letters
- `animation: shine-sweep 2200ms cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1) both` on the heading while `m.playing && !m.reduced`
- Keyframes: `from { background-position: 100% 0; }` to `to { background-position: 0% 0; }`
- The heading holds a real `<button` with `onpointermove={onMove}` and `onkeydown={onKey}` that steer `--x`, the manual takeover (`.shine__title--manual` reads `background-position: var(--x) 0`)
- `@media (forced-colors: active)` falls back to `color: CanvasText`, so the heading stays readable
Motion:
- background-position (a light band across text-clipped gradient): 2200ms cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
