Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #226 "Glass tiers" (foundation/materials).
What it shows: Five glass surfaces at elevations 1 to 5, from Ultra thin to Chrome, over a three color gradient. When to use: Choosing the glass tier by how much blur and fill a panel needs over its background.
Spec:
- `{#each tiers as tier (tier.elevation)}` renders 5 `<Surface material="glass" elevation={tier.elevation} class="tier">`
- Tiers: Ultra thin (`elevation: 1`), Thin (`elevation: 2`), Regular (`elevation: 3`), Thick (`elevation: 4`), Chrome (`elevation: 5`)
- Each tier shows a `label` and a `desc`, for example Subtle background hint and Heaviest blur and fill
- Backdrop gradient uses `var(--ca-brand-main) 0%`, `var(--ca-semantic-info) 50%` and `var(--ca-semantic-success) 100%`
