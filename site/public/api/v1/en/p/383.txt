Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #383 "Before and after slider" (motion/hero-ambient).
What it shows: A before and after compare slider: a handle clips the old design over the new one, and a spring snaps it to 25%, 50% or 75% on release. When to use: Redesign showcases, image or theme comparisons, changelog visuals and onboarding that shows what a setting changes.
Spec:
- `new Spring(50, { stiffness: 0.14, damping: 0.62 })` drives the divider position `pos`
- The before pane is revealed with `clip-path: inset(0 calc(100% - var(--p)) 0 0)`; the handle sits at `inset-inline-start: var(--p)`
- Drag: `{ instant: true }` follows the pointer, and on release `pos.target = nearest(pos.target)` snaps to `snaps` (`[25, 50, 75]`)
- Keyboard: `role="slider"` on the handle; ArrowLeft and ArrowRight move 10, with Shift 25, Home and End jump to 0 and 100
- Autoplay cycles `stops` (`[22, 78, 50]`) every `loop={2200}`; under reduced motion the divider rests at `50%`
Motion:
- clip-path (divider position): spring spring spring { stiffness: 0.14, damping: 0.62 }
