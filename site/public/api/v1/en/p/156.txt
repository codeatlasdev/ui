Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #156 "Open the palette" (navigation/command-palette).
What it shows: A command palette opened by a button, filtering two modules of navigation items and reporting the picked path. When to use: Global quick navigation: jump to any page of a multi-module app by typing its name.
Spec:
- `<CommandPalette {modules} onNavigate={(path) => (picked = path)} bind:open />`
- `const modules: NavModule[]` has two modules, `id: 'app'` with 3 items and `id: 'account'` with 3 items
- Items carry `id`, `label`, `icon` and `path`, for example `path: '/app/dashboard'`
- `<Button variant="filled" onclick={() => (open = true)}>` with `{#snippet leading()}<Keyboard size={16} />{/snippet}` opens it
- A `<Kbd>` hint is shown beside the button
- Result shown in `aria-live="polite"` as `Selected: <code>{picked}</code>`
