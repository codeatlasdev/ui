Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #110 "Sizes" (display/avatar).
What it shows: Avatars at every preset size from extra small to extra large, plus a custom numeric size. When to use: Choosing avatar size for dense lists, headers and profile pages.
Spec:
- `size="xs"` with `alt="Extra small"`
- `size="sm"` with `alt="Small"`
- `size="md"` with `alt="Medium"`
- `size="lg"` with `alt="Large"`
- `size="xl"` with `alt="Extra large"`
- `size={80}` with `alt="Custom"` is a numeric size
