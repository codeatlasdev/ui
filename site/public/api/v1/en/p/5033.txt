Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #5033 "Posts in a feed" (display/clamped-text).
What it shows: Two posts in Cards: a long one cut to 3 lines by ClampedText with a Show more button, and a short one that fits and shows no button. When to use: A feed, comment list or description where long text is cut to a few lines and opened on demand, with no button on text that already fits.
Spec:
- `const posts` has 2 entries with `id`, `author` and `body`: a long paragraph and one short sentence
- Each post is a `<Card>` around an `<article class="post">` with a `<strong class="author">` and `<ClampedText lines={3}>{p.body}</ClampedText>`
- `.feed` is a grid with `gap: var(--ca-space-4)` and `max-width: 560px`
- Only the long post shows the Show more button; it reveals the rest and turns into Show less
