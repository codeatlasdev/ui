Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #430 "Sortable list" (foundation/drag-and-drop).
What it shows: A checklist whose rows reorder by dragging a grip, by touch press-and-hold, or with the keyboard (Space, arrows, Enter). When to use: Any short ordered list the user curates: checklists, playlists, priorities, form field order.
Spec:
- `const dnd = useDnd();` once per component; `{@attach dnd.sortable(() => ({` on the `ul` with `handle: '.sl__grip'`, `getItems` and `onDrop`
- Each `li` carries `data-dnd-item={task.id}`; the grip `button` has `aria-describedby={dnd.instructionsId}` and an `aria-label` naming the task
- `onDrop` commits the move with `applyMove({ list: tasks }, e, (t) => t.id).list`; nothing re-renders while the pointer moves
- `<DndLayer {dnd} />` renders the keyboard instructions (`dnd.instructionsId`) and the drag styles once; every step is announced to screen readers
