Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #946 "Marketing header" (navigation/navigation-menu).
What it shows: A marketing header nav with Products and Resources disclosure buttons that open panels of links (Products links carry a description), followed by Pricing (the current page) and Contact links. When to use: The primary navigation of a marketing site: a few top-level links and a couple of grouped panels, reachable by hover, click and keyboard.
Spec:
- `<NavigationMenu aria-label="Main">` renders a navigation landmark with a list
- `<NavigationMenuItem label="Products">` and `<NavigationMenuItem label="Resources">` are disclosure buttons whose children are `NavigationMenuLink`s
- Products links pass `description` for a second line that is part of the link name
- `<NavigationMenuLink href="#pricing" current>` marks the current page; Contact is a plain link
