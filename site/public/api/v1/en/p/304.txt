Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #304 "Typewriter" (motion/text).
What it shows: A prompt that types itself at 55ms per character, holds for 1100ms, then deletes at 28ms per character while a caret blinks. Typing in the field below takes over the line. When to use: AI and search inputs with example prompts, hero taglines that cycle phrases, terminal and chat demos and onboarding hints.
Spec:
- `MotionStage` from `@codeatlasdev/ui` with `label="Typewriter: a prompt types itself, holds, then deletes"` and `loop={4800}`
- `const TYPE = 55`, `const ERASE = 28` and `const HOLD = 1100` are the typing, deleting and hold times in milliseconds
- One autoplay cycle types `phrases[m.cycle % phrases.length]`, holds it, then deletes it
- `animation: tw-blink 1060ms steps(1, end) infinite` blinks the caret with a hard on/off step
- The typed text and caret are `aria-hidden="true"`; the labelled `id="tw-input"` input is the accessible control
- `bind:value={draft}`: typing in the field replaces the autoplay text, the manual takeover
- Under reduced motion `class:tw__caret--blink={!m.reduced}` stops the blink and the first phrase is shown complete
Motion:
- text content (characters typed, held, then deleted): 55ms per character linear delay 1100ms
- opacity (caret blink): 1060ms steps(1, end)
