Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #199 "Hour by day matrix" (charts/heatmap).
What it shows: A matrix heatmap of task counts by hour of day (24 columns) and weekday (7 rows). When to use: Spotting busy periods in a day-by-hour grid, like support load or traffic.
Spec:
- `<HeatmapChart variant="matrix"`
- `matrixData` is `MatrixHeatmapPoint[]` of `[h, d, Math.max(0, Math.min(10, v))]` triples, 24 hours by 7 days
- `xCategories={hours}` built from `String(i).padStart(2, '0') + ':00'` (24 labels)
- `yCategories={days}` with `'Mon'` to `'Sun'`
- `tooltipFormatter={(v) => `${v} tasks`}`
- `height="320px"` inside `<Card material="glass" full>`
