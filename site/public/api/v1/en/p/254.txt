Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #254 "Chat history" (display/virtual-list).
What it shows: A windowed chat history of 40 messages that loads 30 older ones at the top without moving the view. When to use: Long conversations where older messages load on scroll and the scroll position must be preserved.
Spec:
- `<VirtualList {items} getKey={(m) => m.id} label="Conversation" height="18rem" {hasMore} loadingMore={loading} onloadmore={loadOlder}>`
- `async function loadOlder()` prepends a page of 30 messages with `items = [...page, ...items];`
- `<Button size="sm" variant="outlined" onclick={add}>Receive a message</Button>` appends a new row
