Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #860 "Status tokens" (layout/domain-tokens).
What it shows: Three lead-status pills colored by preset domain tokens, plus a canvas bar per status colored with tokenRgba. When to use: Status, tier or channel colors that must follow the preset and the light or dark scheme: declare them once under tokens in the theme, read them as CSS vars or as [r, g, b, a].
Spec:
- `import { colorScheme, tokenRgba } from '@codeatlasdev/ui'`
- Three `<li class="st__pill st__pill--{s.name}">` pills for `new`, `won` and `lost`, each with `background: var(--ca-token-status-new-subtle)` and `color: var(--ca-token-status-new)` (same pair for `won` and `lost`)
- A `<canvas` of `width="300" height="80"` whose `$effect` reads `colorScheme.resolved` and `colorScheme.preset`, then fills one bar per status with ``tokenRgba(`status.${s.name}`)``
- The pills sit in a wrapping flex row with `gap: var(--ca-space-2)` and `border-radius: var(--ca-radius-pill)`
