Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #206 "Animated proportions" (charts/pie).
What it shows: A donut chart with Q1, Q2 and Q3 chips that switch which quarter of data the chart shows. When to use: Showing how a share breakdown shifts between periods with a selector.
Spec:
- `let selected = $state<Quarter>('Q1')` and `const data = $derived(qData[selected])`
- `{#each quarters as q}` renders `<Chip label={q} selected={selected === q}` with `variant="brand"` and `size="sm"`
- `onclick={() => (selected = q)}` changes the quarter
- `qData` has 4 items per quarter: Cloud, Enterprise, SaaS, Other
- `<PieChart {data} donut height="300px" />` inside `<Card material="glass" full>`
