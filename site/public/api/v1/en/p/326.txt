Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #326 "Scroll-linked counter and chart draw" (motion/scroll).
What it shows: A revenue figure counts up from $0 to $48,210 while a line chart draws itself, both tied to the container's scroll position; scrolling back reverses them. When to use: KPI headers, report sections and pricing pages where a number and its trend should build as the reader reaches them.
Spec:
- `MotionStage` from `@codeatlasdev/ui` with `loop={5000}`: autoplay scrolls the container, a wheel or touch scroll takes over
- The counter text is `Math.round(TOTAL * v)` formatted with `Intl.NumberFormat('en-US')`; `v` is `el.scrollTop / max` (text cannot be scroll-driven in CSS)
- The line has `pathLength="1"` and `stroke-dasharray: 1`; `stroke-dashoffset` goes from `1` to `0` (`calc(1 - var(--p))`)
- Native: `animation-timeline: scroll(nearest block)` with `@keyframes draw`, inside `@supports (animation-timeline: scroll())`; otherwise a `scroll` listener writes `--p`
- Autoplay scrolls in `2800` ms with an ease-out cubic
- Screen readers get the final figure from a `ca-sr-only` span; the animating digits are `aria-hidden="true"`
- Under reduced motion the figure shows the final total and the line is fully drawn
Motion:
- text (count-up 0 to 48,210): scroll position 1 - (1 - t) ** 3
- stroke-dashoffset 1 to 0: animation-timeline: scroll(nearest block) linear
