Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #993 "Viewport simulator" (concepts/viewport).
What it shows: An interactive phone drawing with a device-shape selector, switches for collapsed browser bars and edge-to-edge drawing, a keyboard height slider and a keyboard resize mode, plus a table of what 100lvh, 100svh and 100dvh resolve to and how much of each is hidden. When to use: Teaching why a full-height block must be a floor (min-height) and not a fixed height, and how safe areas and the virtual keyboard change the usable height.
Spec:
- `import { DEVICE_PRESETS, hiddenBy, type PresetName, viewportHeights } from './model';` computes the numbers; the model is pure and unit-tested
- `<SegmentedControl` with `aria-label="Device shape"` and `bind:value={preset}` offers four presets: narrow phone, typical phone, short phone, phone in landscape
- `<Switch bind:checked={collapsed} label="Browser bars collapsed (after scrolling)" />` and `<Switch bind:checked={edgeToEdge} label="Page draws edge to edge (viewport-fit=cover)" />`
- `<Slider bind:value={keyboard}` for the keyboard height (`step={10}`), plus a second `SegmentedControl` with `aria-label="Keyboard resize mode"` for `resizes-content` or `resizes-visual`
- A to-scale phone `div` with `role="img"` and an `aria-label`, drawn with CSS custom properties (`--w`, `--h`, `--bar`, `--vis`, `--kb`), and a `<table class="sim__table">` with a screen-reader caption listing 100lvh, 100svh, 100dvh and the usable height
