Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #721 "Required choice with error" (inputs/chip-group).
What it shows: ChipGroup inside a native form with a required choice and a field error shown after submitting without a selection. When to use: A form step with one required single choice, where the error must be announced and the value must reach FormData through `name`.
Spec:
- `<ChipGroup` with `label="Sales team size"`, `name="teamSize"`, `items={sizes}`, `bind:value={size}`, `{error}` and `required`
- `sizes` has 3 items: `1`, `2-5`, `6-20`
- `let size = $state('')` starts with nothing selected
- `onsubmit` prevents default and sets the error to `'Choose a team size.'` when `size` is empty
- `onchange` clears the error
- `<Button type="submit" variant="filled">Continue</Button>` submits the form
