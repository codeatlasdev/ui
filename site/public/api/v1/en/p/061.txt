Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #061 "Variants and orientation" (inputs/toggle-group).
What it shows: Three toggle groups sharing one value: outlined, ghost and vertical. When to use: Choosing a view such as list, grid or board with a button-style group.

- Outlined items: List, Grid, Board, and Board has `disabled: true`
- Ghost group: `variant="ghost"`, `size="sm"`, `aria-label="Ghost"`
- Vertical group: `orientation="vertical"`, `aria-label="Vertical"`
- All 3 use `bind:value={view}` with initial `$state('list')`
