Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #720 "Wrapping chips" (inputs/chip-group).
What it shows: Single-choice chips for a sales team size: five short options that wrap to two rows on a phone. When to use: Choosing one of a few short options (team size, plan length, frequency) where RadioGroup is too tall and SegmentedControl would overflow a 390px screen.
Spec:
- `<ChipGroup label="Sales team size" items={sizes} bind:value={size} />`
- `sizes` has 5 items: `1` Just me, `2-5` 2 to 5, `6-20` 6 to 20, `21-50` 21 to 50, `50+` 50+
- `bind:value={size}` with initial `$state('2-5')`; the selected chip shows a check icon
