Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #162 "Horizontal stepper" (navigation/stepper).
What it shows: A four-step horizontal stepper inside a glass card with Back and Next buttons that advance the step. When to use: Multi-step flows such as onboarding or checkout where the user moves through ordered stages.
Spec:
- `<Stepper {steps} bind:current={step} />` with `let step = $state(0)`
- `const steps` has 4 entries with a `label` field: Account, Profile, Billing, Review
- Wrapper is `<Card material="glass" p="6" full>` with `<Stack gap="6">`
- Body shows `Step {step + 1}: {steps[step].label}`
- Back is `<Button variant="ghost" disabled={step <= 0} onclick={() => step--}>`
- Next is `<Button variant="filled" disabled={step >= steps.length - 1} onclick={() => step++}>` and reads Finish on the last step
