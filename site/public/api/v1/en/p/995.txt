Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #995 "A form with the keyboard open" (concepts/viewport).
What it shows: A scrollable account form with three TextFields (work email, company, role) and a StickyActions bar with a stretched Continue button, wrapped in the keyboardAware action. When to use: A form that stays operable with the on-screen keyboard open: the page scrolls, the focused field comes into view and the action bar rides above the keyboard and the safe area.
Spec:
- `<form` with `use:keyboardAware` (from `@codeatlasdev/ui/actions`), `max-height: 24rem` and `overflow-y: auto`
- `<TextField label="Work email" type="email" bind:value={email} autocomplete="email" />`, then `Company` and `Role` fields with matching `autocomplete`
- A filler `div` of at least `8rem` so the form scrolls
- `<StickyActions align="stretch">` holds `<Button variant="filled" type="submit">` whose label becomes `Sent` after submit
