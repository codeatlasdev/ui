Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #111 "Avatar stack" (display/avatar).
What it shows: An overlapping avatar stack of three photo avatars and a fourth `+4` overflow avatar. When to use: Showing collaborators on a card, project or document with an overflow count.
Spec:
- `const people` has 4 entries: Alice, Bob and Clara with `src`, and `{ alt: '+4' }` without one
- `{#each people as p, i}` renders `<Avatar src={p.src} alt={p.alt} size="md" />`
- Each avatar is wrapped in `<div class="item" style="--z:{people.length - i}">`
- Overlap uses `.item { margin-left: -10px; position: relative; z-index: var(--z, 0); }`
