Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #133 "Basic" (display/pagination).
What it shows: Pagination for 100 items at 10 per page, with the current page bound to state and printed below. When to use: Paged lists and tables where the app needs to know the active page to fetch or slice data.
Spec:
- `<Pagination count={100} perPage={10} bind:page />`
- State: `let page = $state(1)` starts on page 1
- A paragraph renders `Current page: {page}` below the control
