Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #100 "In a button" (feedback/spinner).
What it shows: A Button that shows its loading spinner for two seconds after click and changes its label to Saving. When to use: Save or submit buttons that wait on a request.
Spec:
- `<Button onclick={simulate} {loading}>` with `let loading = $state(false)`
- `simulate` sets `loading = true` and resets it with `setTimeout(() => (loading = false), 2000)`
- Label is `{loading ? 'Saving…' : 'Save changes'}`
