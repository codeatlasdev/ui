Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #020 "Basic" (inputs/file-upload).
What it shows: A multi-file FileUpload with a size limit and hint that reports how many files are selected. When to use: Attachment and document upload areas that accept several files and cap their size.
Spec:
- `bind:files` with `let files = $state<File[]>([])`
- `multiple`
- `hint="PNG, JPG, PDF up to 10 MB"`
- `maxSize={10 * 1024 * 1024}`
- `{#if files.length > 0}` shows the selected file count with a plural `s`
- Wrapper `.wrap` has `max-width: 480px`
