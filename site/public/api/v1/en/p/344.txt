Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #344 "Hold to confirm" (motion/micro).
What it shows: A destructive button that only fires after a 1.2 second hold, filling a progress ring; releasing early cancels and drains the ring. Space and Enter work the same way. When to use: Deleting data, sending money or any action that must be deliberate without a confirmation dialog.
Spec:
- `MotionStage` from `@codeatlasdev/ui` with `loop={3400}`
- `const HOLD = 1200;` milliseconds to hold; progress is `(now - start) / HOLD` read in `requestAnimationFrame`
- `stroke-dashoffset={C * (1 - (m.reduced && !done ? 0 : progress))}` on the ring circle, with `C = 2 * Math.PI * R`
- `onkeydown` starts on Space or Enter (`!e.repeat`), `onkeyup` and `onblur` call `cancel`
- `onpointerup`, `onpointerleave` and `onpointercancel` call `cancel`, which sets `progress = 0`
- `announce('Project deleted')` tells assistive tech when the hold completes; Undo resets
Motion:
- stroke-dashoffset (progress ring): 1200ms linear
