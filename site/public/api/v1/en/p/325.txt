Spec:
- `MotionStage` from `@codeatlasdev/ui` with `loop={5600}`: autoplay scrolls the container, a wheel or touch scroll takes over

- Inactive steps use `opacity: 0.4`, the active step `opacity: 1`
- Under reduced motion the last step is active and nothing scrolls by itself
