Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #5025 "Inbox page" (layout/master-detail).
What it shows: A complete inbox: ConversationItem rows in the master, a MessageBubble thread with a reply field in the detail, an empty state until a row is picked. When to use: The messages screen of a CRM or helpdesk: two columns when wide, and on a phone the thread opens as a full-screen pane with Back.
Spec:
- `<MasterDetail bind:selected detailTitle={current?.name ?? ''} listWidth="18rem" masterLabel={m.list}>`
- `<ConversationItem title={t.name} time={t.time} preview={last(t)} unread={t.unread} active={selected === t.id}`
- `<MessageBubble direction={msg.mine ? 'out' : 'in'} time={msg.time}>`
- `<EmptyState title={m.empty} />`
