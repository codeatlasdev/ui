Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #365 "Sheet spring" (motion/transitions).
What it shows: A bottom sheet that springs up over its page, follows your finger when dragged down and either dismisses or springs back depending on distance and speed. When to use: Mobile pickers, filters, share menus and any secondary task that should feel attached to the screen instead of popping in.
Spec:
- `MotionStage` with `label="Sheet spring: a bottom sheet springs up and drags down to dismiss"` and `loop={2600}`
- `new Spring(SHEET, { stiffness: 0.2, damping: 0.75 })` with `const SHEET = 256`: offset 0 is open, `SHEET` is hidden
- The sheet reads `transform: translateY(var(--y))`; the scrim opacity follows `calc(var(--o) * 0.5)`
- Drag down follows the pointer 1:1 with `{ instant: true }`; dragging up divides by `4` (rubber band)
- Release dismisses past `SHEET * 0.3` or faster than `0.5` px/ms, otherwise the spring returns to 0; Escape also closes it
- Under reduced motion the sheet is open and moves with `{ instant: true }`
Motion:
- transform: translateY of the sheet: stiffness: 0.2 damping: 0.75 spring { stiffness: 0.2, damping: 0.75 }
Names: Spring sheet | Bottom sheet drag to dismiss | Swipe to dismiss | Sheet com mola e arrastar para fechar | Hoja con resorte y arrastrar para cerrar
