Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #961 "Embed" (display/aspect-ratio).
What it shows: An AspectRatio of 16/9 inside a 28rem frame holding a centred caption, standing in for a video or iframe. When to use: Embeds, maps and video players that have no intrinsic size but must hold a ratio while loading.
Spec:
- `<AspectRatio ratio={16 / 9}>` with one `<div class="slot">16 / 9: any child, one box</div>` child
- Wrapper `.frame` is `max-width: 28rem` with `--ca-radius-md` and `overflow: hidden`
- `.slot` is `height: 100%` on `--ca-surface-sunken` and centres its text
