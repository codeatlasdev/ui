Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #009 "Without label" (inputs/checkbox).
What it shows: Three checkboxes with no visible label, named only through aria-label: bound, checked and indeterminate. When to use: Row selection checkboxes in tables or lists where the label is implied by the row and only an accessible name is needed.
Spec:
- `bind:checked={value}` with `aria-label="Unlabelled checkbox"`
- `checked` with `aria-label="Unlabelled checked checkbox"`
- `indeterminate` with `aria-label="Unlabelled indeterminate checkbox"`
- No `label` prop on any of the three
