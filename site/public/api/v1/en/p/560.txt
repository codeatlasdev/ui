Use @codeatlasdev/ui demo #560 "Reading width with an aside" (layout/page-shell).
What it shows: A PageShell with width="reading" (form column capped at 46rem) and an aside snippet holding a section index, inside a fixed-height frame. When to use: Settings and form pages on wide screens that need a readable column plus a companion column (in-page index, summary, help, preview).
Spec:
- Frame is a 420px tall flex column with an inset outline
- `<PageShell title="Workspace settings" description="Reading width keeps the form at a comfortable line length." width="reading" asideLabel="Sections">`
- `{#snippet aside()}` renders a glass `Card` with a nav (aria-label On this page) of three links: General, Billing, Danger zone
- Body is a `Card material="glass" full title="General"` with a `Stack gap="4"` of two `TextField`s: Name (Acme Inc.) and Slug (acme)
