What it shows: A DataTable driven from outside: filter, sort and pagination state live in the page, which slices 12 users into pages of 5. When to use: Server-side or externally managed tables where the page owns search, sort and paging state.

- `createCaColumnHelper<User>()` defines 5 columns with `col.accessor`
- `onGlobalFilterChange` resets `pageIndex: 0`
