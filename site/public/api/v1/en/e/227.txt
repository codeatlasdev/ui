Element: #227 Tinted and glowing (foundation/materials)
What it shows: Three glass surfaces over a gradient: tinted, glowing, and tinted with glow.
When to use: Highlighting a glass chip or panel with a brand tint, a glow, or both.
Spec:
- `<Surface material="glass" tone="brand" class="chip">` labelled Tinted
- `<Surface material="glass" glow class="chip">` labelled Glowing
- `<Surface material="glass" tone="brand" glow class="chip">` labelled Tinted + glowing
- Backdrop gradient runs `var(--ca-brand-main) 0%` to `var(--ca-semantic-success) 100%`
Tags: materials, glass, surface, tone, glow, tinted
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Surface
