Element: #642 Stats in a card (layout/kpi-card)
What it shows: A glass Card titled This week holding three card-less Stat blocks in a responsive grid: leads with a hint, reply rate with a unit, trend and hint, and booked calls with a positive trend.
When to use: Plain statistics inside an existing card or panel, with no nested box, instead of several KpiCards.
Spec:
- `<Card material="glass" title="This week" headingLevel={3} class="stats-card">` with `max-width: 640px`
- `<Stat label="Leads this week" value="128" hint="12 more than last week" />`
- `<Stat label="Reply rate" value="18" unit="%" trend={-2.4} hint="Goal: 25%" />`
- `<Stat label="Booked calls" value="9" trend={12.5} />`
- The Stats sit in an auto-fit grid, `minmax(150px, 1fr)`, `gap: var(--ca-space-5)`
Tags: stat, kpi, metric, statistic, dashboard, card-less, hint, trend
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.5.0
Components: Card, Stat
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/layout/kpi-card#stats-in-a-card
