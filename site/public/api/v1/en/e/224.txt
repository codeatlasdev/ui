Element: #224 Text (foundation/colors)
What it shows: Four swatches for the text color tokens: primary, secondary, hint and disabled.
When to use: Choosing a text color by importance, from main content to hints and disabled labels.

- Tokens in order: `--ca-text-primary`, `--ca-text-secondary`, `--ca-text-hint`, `--ca-text-disabled`
