Element: #094 Indeterminate (feedback/progress)
What it shows: Two indeterminate progress bars labelled Loading and Syncing for work of unknown length.
When to use: Background loading or syncing where no percentage is available.
Spec:
- `<Progress indeterminate label="Loading content" />`
- `<Progress indeterminate label="Syncing data" />`
- Each bar sits in a row next to a text label, Loading and Syncing
- Column has `max-width: 480px`
Tags: progress, indeterminate, loading, sync, label
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Progress
