Element: #013 Icon only and toast (inputs/copy-button)
What it shows: An icon-only CopyButton and a labelled one that shows a toast after copying, with a Toaster mounted.
When to use: Copying links or snippets where a compact icon is enough or where a visible confirmation toast is wanted.
Spec:
- `<Toaster />` is rendered alongside the buttons
- `<CopyButton value="icon-only" label="" />` is icon-only via an empty `label`
- `<CopyButton value="https://ui.codeatlas.com.br" label="Copy link" toast="Link copied" />`
Tags: copy-button, toast, toaster, icon-only, clipboard, label
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: CopyButton, Toaster
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/inputs/copy-button#icon-only-and-toast
