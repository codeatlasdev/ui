Element: #107 With image (display/avatar)
What it shows: A row of four large avatars loaded from image URLs.
When to use: User lists, comments and team pages where members have profile photos.
Spec:
- Four avatars with `size="lg"`
- `src="https://i.pravatar.cc/150?img=1"` with `alt="Alice Martin"`
- `src="https://i.pravatar.cc/150?img=12"` with `alt="Bob Chen"`
- `src="https://i.pravatar.cc/150?img=32"` with `alt="Clara Silva"`
- `src="https://i.pravatar.cc/150?img=47"` with `alt="David Kim"`
Tags: avatar, image, src, alt, profile-photo
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Avatar
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/display/avatar#with-image
