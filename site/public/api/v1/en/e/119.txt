Element: #119 StatusChip (display/chip)
What it shows: StatusChip in its five statuses: success, warning, error, info and neutral.
When to use: Showing the state of a record, such as active, pending, failed, scheduled or draft, in tables and lists.
Spec:
- `<StatusChip status="success" label="Active" />`
- `<StatusChip status="warning" label="Pending" />`
- `<StatusChip status="error" label="Failed" />`
- `<StatusChip status="info" label="Scheduled" />`
- `<StatusChip status="neutral" label="Draft" />`
