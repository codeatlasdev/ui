Element: #052 Settings list (inputs/switch)
What it shows: Settings list of three rows, each with a title, a subtitle and a Switch.
When to use: Account preferences screen with one toggle per setting.
Spec:
- 3 rows in `.settings-list`: Email notifications, Dark mode, Usage analytics
- Each row has `settings-item__title` and `settings-item__sub` text
- Switches use `bind:checked={email}`, `bind:checked={dark}`, `bind:checked={analytics}`
- Each switch has an `aria-label` matching its row title, e.g. `aria-label="Dark mode"`
- Initial state: email `$state(true)`, dark and analytics `$state(false)`
Tags: switch, settings-list, preferences, aria-label, toggle
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Switch
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/inputs/switch#settings-list

<script lang="ts">
  import { Switch } from '@codeatlasdev/ui';

  let email = $state(true);
  let dark = $state(false);
  let analytics = $state(false);
</script>

<div class="settings-list">
  <div class="settings-item">
    <div>
      <p class="settings-item__title">Email notifications</p>
      <p class="settings-item__sub">Receive updates about your account activity.</p>
    </div>
    <Switch bind:checked={email} aria-label="Email notifications" />
  </div>
  <div class="settings-item">
    <div>
      <p class="settings-item__title">Dark mode</p>
      <p class="settings-item__sub">Switch between light and dark appearance.</p>
    </div>
    <Switch bind:checked={dark} aria-label="Dark mode" />
  </div>
  <div class="settings-item">
    <div>
      <p class="settings-item__title">Usage analytics</p>
      <p class="settings-item__sub">Help improve the product by sharing usage data.</p>
    </div>
    <Switch bind:checked={analytics} aria-label="Usage analytics" />
  </div>
</div>
