Element: #032 Default (inputs/pin-input)
What it shows: A verification-code PinInput that reports its value and completion, plus a masked four-digit PIN.
When to use: One-time code entry for sign-in verification, or a short secret PIN that must be hidden.
Spec:
- `<PinInput label="Verification code" bind:value={code} oncomplete={(v) => (done = v)} />`
- Output line prints `value: {code || '–'} · completed: {done || '–'}`
- `<PinInput label="PIN" length={4} mask bind:value={pin} />`
Tags: pin-input, otp, verification-code, mask, oncomplete, forms
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: PinInput
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/inputs/pin-input#default
