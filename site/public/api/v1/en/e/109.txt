Element: #109 Shape and status (display/avatar)
What it shows: Large avatars showing the rounded shape and the four status indicators: online, busy, away and offline.
When to use: Presence in chat, team lists or support queues, plus a rounded avatar shape.
Spec:
- `shape="rounded"` on `alt="Ana Lima"`
- `status="online"` on `alt="Online"`
- `status="busy"` on `alt="Busy"`
- `status="away"` on `alt="Away"`
- `status="offline"` on `alt="Offline"`
- All five use `size="lg"`
Tags: avatar, shape, rounded, status, presence, online
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Avatar
