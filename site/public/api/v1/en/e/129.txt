Element: #129 Shortcuts in text (display/kbd)
What it shows: Keyboard key caps inline in a sentence describing the Ctrl+K shortcut to open the command palette and Esc to close it.
When to use: Documenting keyboard shortcuts inside help text, tooltips or onboarding hints.
Spec:
- `<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>` and `<Kbd>K</Kbd>` joined by a plus sign inside a paragraph
- `<Kbd>Esc</Kbd>` as the second shortcut in the same sentence
- Each key is plain text children of `Kbd`, no props
Tags: kbd, keyboard, shortcut, key, command-palette, hint
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Kbd
