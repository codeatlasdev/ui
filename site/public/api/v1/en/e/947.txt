Element: #947 Delays and alignment (navigation/navigation-menu)
What it shows: A two-item docs nav (Guides, Reference) that opens on hover at once and stays open 400 ms after the pointer leaves.
When to use: Tuning how eager the hover behaviour is for dense navigation, and aligning each panel to its button's start or end.
Spec:
- `<NavigationMenu aria-label="Docs sections" openDelay={0} closeDelay={400}>`
- `<NavigationMenuItem label="Guides" align="start">` and `<NavigationMenuItem label="Reference" align="end">` align their panels to the button
- Each panel holds two `NavigationMenuLink`s
Tags: navigation-menu, delay, hover, align, docs, disclosure
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.5.0
Components: NavigationMenu, NavigationMenuItem, NavigationMenuLink
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/navigation/navigation-menu#delays-and-alignment

<script lang="ts">
  import { NavigationMenu, NavigationMenuItem, NavigationMenuLink } from '@codeatlasdev/ui';
</script>

<NavigationMenu aria-label="Docs sections" openDelay={0} closeDelay={400}>
  <NavigationMenuItem label="Guides" align="start">
    <NavigationMenuLink href="#start">Getting started</NavigationMenuLink>
    <NavigationMenuLink href="#theming">Theming</NavigationMenuLink>
  </NavigationMenuItem>
  <NavigationMenuItem label="Reference" align="end">
    <NavigationMenuLink href="#components">Components</NavigationMenuLink>
    <NavigationMenuLink href="#tokens">Tokens</NavigationMenuLink>
  </NavigationMenuItem>
</NavigationMenu>
