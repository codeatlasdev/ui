Element: #054 Basic (inputs/text-area)
What it shows: Three text areas: empty, with a placeholder, and with initial content.
When to use: Free-form multi-line input such as a message, notes or a description.
Spec:
- `<TextArea label="Message" bind:value={message} />` starts empty
- `<TextArea label="Notes" placeholder="Write something…" bind:value={notes} />`
- `<TextArea label="Description" bind:value={description} />` starts as `Some initial content here.`
Tags: text-area, textarea, placeholder, label, bind-value
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: TextArea
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/inputs/text-area#basic
