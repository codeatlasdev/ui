Element: #051 Disabled (inputs/switch)
What it shows: Two disabled switches, one off and one on.
When to use: Showing a setting that cannot be changed, for example a locked plan feature.
Spec:
- `<Switch disabled label="Disabled off" />`
- `<Switch checked disabled label="Disabled on" />`
- No bound state
Tags: switch, disabled, checked, toggle, state
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Switch
