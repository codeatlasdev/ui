Element: #172 404 without actions (layout/error-page)
What it shows: The ErrorPage component rendering a 404 status without its action buttons.
When to use: Route-level error screens for not found, forbidden or server errors.
Spec:
- `<ErrorPage status={404} hideActions />`
- Only the `status` prop and `hideActions` are passed, no custom title or actions

Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
