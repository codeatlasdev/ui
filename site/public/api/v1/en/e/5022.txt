Element: #5022 Flush in a panel (surfaces/collapsible)
What it shows: Two flush Collapsibles inside a padded side panel: no box, title and body aligned to the panel gutter, a hairline between sections.
When to use: Sections inside a surface that already has its own background, border and padding, such as a context side panel.
Spec:
- `<Collapsible title="Contact" variant="flush" open>`
- `<Collapsible title="Deal" variant="flush" divided open>` draws a top border
- Wrapped in `<aside class="panel">` with `padding: 0 var(--ca-space-4)` and `max-width: 360px`
Tags: collapsible, flush, panel, divided, sidebar
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.6.0
Components: Collapsible
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/surfaces/collapsible#flush-in-a-panel

</script>

  </Collapsible>
  <Collapsible title="Deal" variant="flush" divided open>
    <p>divided draws the hairline between sections.</p>
  </Collapsible>
</aside>
