Element: #5018 Rating (inputs/rating)
What it shows: Star ratings: an editable whole-star rating, a half-step rating and a small read-only average.
When to use: Collecting or showing a score out of five, such as service or product reviews.
Spec:
- A `Rating` labelled `Service` bound to 4
- A `Rating` labelled `Food` with `step={0.5}` bound to 3.5
- A `readonly` `size="sm"` `Rating` labelled `Average` showing 4.5
Tags: rating, stars, review, score, radiogroup, half steps
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.6.0
Components: Rating
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/inputs/rating#rating

<script lang="ts">
  import { Rating } from '@codeatlasdev/ui';

  let service = $state(4);
  let food = $state(3.5);
</script>

<div class="ratings">
  <Rating label="Service" bind:value={service} />
  <Rating label="Food" step={0.5} bind:value={food} />
  <Rating label="Average" readonly step={0.5} value={4.5} size="sm" />
</div>
