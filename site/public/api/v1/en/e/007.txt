Element: #007 Disabled (inputs/checkbox)
What it shows: Three disabled checkboxes: unchecked, checked and indeterminate.
When to use: Showing a read-only option whose value the user cannot change, such as a setting locked by plan or permission.
Spec:
- `disabled` with `label="Disabled unchecked"`
- `checked disabled` with `label="Disabled checked"`
- `indeterminate disabled` with `label="Disabled indeterminate"`
- Three `<Checkbox` components, no bound state
Tags: checkbox, disabled, checked, indeterminate, form
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Checkbox
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/inputs/checkbox#disabled

</script>
