Element: #543 Paper grain (surfaces/surface)
What it shows: Three solid Surface tiles: static paper grain, animated paper grain and no grain.
When to use: Giving a card or panel a printed-paper texture for editorial and marketing layouts.
Spec:
- `<Surface material="solid" elevation={2} grain p="4" class="tile">` is the static grain
- `grain="animated"` shifts the noise and stays still under reduced motion
- The third tile has no `grain` prop
Tags: surface, grain, paper, noise, texture, editorial
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.5.0
Components: Surface
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/surfaces/surface#paper-grain
