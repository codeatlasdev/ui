Element: #068 Tone and glow (surfaces/card)
What it shows: Two glass Cards side by side: one with a brand tint and one with a brand glow, each with a title and one line of text.
When to use: Highlighting a featured or selected card without changing its content layout.
Spec:
- `<Card material="glass" tone="brand" class="accent">` titled Tinted
- `<Card material="glass" glow class="accent">` titled Glowing
- Both cards share `class="accent"` styled with `flex: 1` and `min-width: 200px`
- Card content is a `.title` paragraph and a `.text` paragraph
Tags: card, glass, tone, brand, glow, accent
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Card
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/surfaces/card#tone-and-glow
