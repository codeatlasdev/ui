Element: #223 Surfaces (foundation/colors)
What it shows: Four swatches for the surface tokens from sunken to overlay, drawn with a border.
When to use: Choosing the background layer for a page, card, raised panel or popover.
Spec:
- Renders `<Swatches {tokens} bordered={true} />` with 4 tokens
- Tokens in order: `--ca-surface-sunken`, `--ca-surface-base`, `--ca-surface-raised`, `--ca-surface-overlay`

Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
