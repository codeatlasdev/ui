Element: #070 Header and actions (surfaces/card)
What it shows: A glass Card with a built-in Team members header and an Invite button supplied through a headerActions snippet.
When to use: Panels whose header needs a title and an action, such as team, billing or settings sections.
Spec:
- `<Card material="glass" title="Team members" class="titled">` with `max-width: 420px`
- `{#snippet headerActions()}` renders `<Button size="sm" variant="outlined">Invite</Button>`
- Body is one paragraph explaining `title` and `headerActions`
Tags: card, header-actions, title, snippet, button, glass
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Card, Button
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/surfaces/card#header-and-actions

<script lang="ts">
  import { Card, Button } from '@codeatlasdev/ui';
</script>

<Card material="glass" title="Team members" class="titled">
  {#snippet headerActions()}<Button size="sm" variant="outlined">Invite</Button>{/snippet}
  <p>Use <code>title</code> and <code>headerActions</code> for a built-in card header.</p>
</Card>
