Element: #004 Sizes (inputs/button)
What it shows: The three button sizes side by side: small, medium and large.
When to use: Choosing button density: `sm` in toolbars and tables, `md` by default, `lg` for hero or touch-first actions.
Spec:
- `size="sm"` labelled Small
- `size="md"` labelled Medium
- `size="lg"` labelled Large
