Element: #098 Image placeholder (feedback/skeleton)
What it shows: A flush glass card skeleton with a 180px rectangular image placeholder above two caption lines.
When to use: Loading placeholder for media cards such as products, posts or galleries.
Spec:
- `<Card material="glass" flush full>`
- `<Skeleton variant="rectangular" height="180px" />` fills the top of the card
- Caption in `<Stack gap="2">`: `height="16px" width="50%"` and `height="13px" width="80%"`
- Caption wrapper `<div class="caption">` has `padding: 16px`
Tags: skeleton, image, card, flush, loading, placeholder
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Skeleton, Card, Stack
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/feedback/skeleton#image-placeholder

<script lang="ts">
  import { Skeleton, Card, Stack } from '@codeatlasdev/ui';
</script>

<Card material="glass" flush full>
  <Skeleton variant="rectangular" height="180px" />
  <div class="caption">
    <Stack gap="2">
      <Skeleton height="16px" width="50%" />
      <Skeleton height="13px" width="80%" />
    </Stack>
  </div>
</Card>

<style>
  .caption { padding: 16px; }
</style>
