Element: #221 Outline (foundation/colors)
What it shows: Three swatches for the outline tokens, drawn with a border so they stay visible on the page.
When to use: Choosing a divider or border strength from the subtle, default and strong outline tokens.
Spec:
- Renders `<Swatches {tokens} bordered={true} />` with 3 tokens
- Tokens in order: `--ca-outline-subtle`, `--ca-outline-default`, `--ca-outline-strong`
Tags: colors, outline, border, tokens, swatches
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: none
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/foundation/colors#outline

<script lang="ts">
	import Swatches from "./Swatches.svelte";

</script>
