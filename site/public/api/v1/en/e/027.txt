Element: #027 Currency and locale (inputs/money-input)
What it shows: MoneyInput in three currencies and locales: USD en-US, JPY without decimals and EUR de-DE.
When to use: Price fields in multi-currency products that must format and parse per locale.
Spec:
- `currency="USD" locale="en-US"` with `bind:value={usd}` starting at `$state(123456)`
- `currency="JPY" locale="en-US"` with `bind:value={jpy}` starting at `$state(1234)`
- `currency="EUR" locale="de-DE"` with `bind:value={eur}` starting at `$state(123456)`
- Labels: `Price (USD, en-US)`, `Price (JPY, no decimals)` and `Preis (EUR, de-DE)`
Tags: money-input, currency, locale, usd, jpy, eur, forms
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: MoneyInput
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/inputs/money-input#currency-and-locale

<script lang="ts">
  import { MoneyInput } from '@codeatlasdev/ui/forms';

  let usd = $state(123456);
  let jpy = $state(1234);
  let eur = $state(123456);
</script>

<div class="col">
  <MoneyInput label="Price (USD, en-US)" currency="USD" locale="en-US" bind:value={usd} />
  <MoneyInput label="Price (JPY, no decimals)" currency="JPY" locale="en-US" bind:value={jpy} />
  <MoneyInput label="Preis (EUR, de-DE)" currency="EUR" locale="de-DE" bind:value={eur} />
</div>
