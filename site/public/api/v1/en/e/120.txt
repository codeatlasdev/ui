Element: #120 Variants and size (display/chip)
What it shows: Chip color variants (default, brand, success, warning, error, info) plus a small size chip.
When to use: Picking the chip tone for categories and semantic labels.
Spec:
- `<Chip label="Default" />` has no variant
- `variant="brand"`, `variant="success"`, `variant="warning"`, `variant="error"` and `variant="info"` each with a matching label
- `<Chip label="Small" size="sm" />` shows the small size
Tags: chip, variant, brand, size, sm, color
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Chip
