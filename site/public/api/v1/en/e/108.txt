Element: #108 Initials fallback (display/avatar)
What it shows: Large avatars with no image source: three given only `alt` names and two given explicit `fallback` text.
When to use: Users who have no uploaded photo, shown with initials or a placeholder character.
Spec:
- `alt="Alice Martin"`, `alt="Bob Chen"` and `alt="Clara Silva"` with no image
- `fallback="DK"` sets explicit text
- `fallback="?"` sets a placeholder character
- All five use `size="lg"`
Tags: avatar, initials, fallback, alt, placeholder
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Avatar
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/display/avatar#initials-fallback
