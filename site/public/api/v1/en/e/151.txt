Element: #151 Default (navigation/app-bar)
What it shows: A glass app bar with a title on the left and search, notifications and avatar on the right, inside a flush glass card.
When to use: Top bar of a page or panel with a product title and a few global quick actions.
Spec:
- `<Card material="glass" full flush>` wraps the bar
- `GlassAppBar` content is `<span class="title">My Application</span>`
- `{#snippet trailing()}` holds two `IconButton` items with `aria-label="Search"` and `aria-label="Notifications"`
- Trailing also has `<Avatar alt="Ada L" size="sm" />`
- Icons are `<Search size={18} />` and `<Bell size={18} />` from `@lucide/svelte`
Tags: app-bar, glass-app-bar, header, toolbar, icon-button, avatar
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: GlassAppBar, IconButton, Avatar, Card
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/navigation/app-bar#default

<script lang="ts">
  import { GlassAppBar, IconButton, Avatar, Card } from '@codeatlasdev/ui';
  import { Search, Bell } from '@lucide/svelte';
</script>

<Card material="glass" full flush>
  <GlassAppBar>
    <span class="title">My Application</span>
    {#snippet trailing()}
      <IconButton aria-label="Search"><Search size={18} /></IconButton>
      <IconButton aria-label="Notifications"><Bell size={18} /></IconButton>
      <Avatar alt="Ada L" size="sm" />
    {/snippet}
  </GlassAppBar>
</Card>

<style>
  .title { font-weight: 600; color: var(--ca-text-primary); }
</style>
