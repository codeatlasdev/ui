Element: #069 Content card (surfaces/card)
What it shows: A single glass Card with a Featured eyebrow, a heading, a description and an Explore materials link.
When to use: Promo or feature cards in a landing page or dashboard that lead to a detail page.
Spec:
- `<Card material="glass" class="content-card">` with `max-width: 360px`
- Body is a `<div class="body">` with an eyebrow paragraph, `<h3>Liquid Glass materials</h3>`, a description paragraph and a link
- Link is `<a href="/docs/foundation/materials/">Explore materials →</a>`
- The card itself has no `href`, only the inner anchor is a link
