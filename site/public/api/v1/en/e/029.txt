Element: #029 Helpers (inputs/money-input)
What it shows: Two money helper functions evaluated inline: centsToDisplay and parseInputToCents.
When to use: Formatting stored cents for display or parsing user-typed currency strings outside a MoneyInput.
Spec:
- `centsToDisplay(123456)` is printed with its result
- `parseInputToCents('R$ 1.234,56')` is printed with its result
- Both helpers are imported from `@codeatlasdev/ui/forms`
- No input component is rendered
Tags: money-input, helpers, cents, parse, format, currency
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: none
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/inputs/money-input#helpers

<script lang="ts">
  import { centsToDisplay, parseInputToCents } from '@codeatlasdev/ui/forms';
</script>

<div class="col">
  <p class="out">centsToDisplay(123456) = {centsToDisplay(123456)}</p>
  <p class="out">parseInputToCents("R$ 1.234,56") = {parseInputToCents('R$ 1.234,56')}</p>
</div>
