Element: #180 Container and Section (layout/layout-primitives)
What it shows: A Container and Section holding a Stack of two plan cards, showing the page-level layout primitives.
When to use: Structuring a marketing or settings page with a centered container, a labelled section and stacked cards.
Spec:
- `<Container size="md">` wraps `<Section size="1" aria-label="Plans">`
- `<Stack gap="4">` holds 2 cards with `material="solid"` and `headingLevel={3}`
- Cards are `title="Starter"` (Up to 3 projects.) and `title="Team"` (Unlimited projects and roles.)
Tags: container, section, stack, card, layout, page, heading-level
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Container, Section, Stack, Card
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/layout/layout-primitives#container-and-section
