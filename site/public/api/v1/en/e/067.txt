Element: #067 Single (surfaces/accordion)
What it shows: A single-open Accordion with three Liquid Glass FAQ questions, where opening one closes the others.
When to use: FAQ sections where only one answer should be visible at a time.
Spec:
- `<Accordion type="single">`
- 3 items: `<AccordionItem value="a1" title="What is Liquid Glass?">`, `value="a2"` and `value="a3"`
- Item titles: What is Liquid Glass?, Which browsers support backdrop-filter?, Can I nest a Surface inside a Card?
- The accordion is uncontrolled, nothing is bound
- Wrapped in `.wrap` with `max-width: 560px`
Tags: accordion, accordion-item, single, faq, disclosure
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.1.0
Components: Accordion, AccordionItem
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/surfaces/accordion#single

<script lang="ts">
  import { Accordion, AccordionItem } from '@codeatlasdev/ui';
</script>

<div class="wrap">
  <Accordion type="single">
    <AccordionItem value="a1" title="What is Liquid Glass?">
      Liquid Glass is the opt-in look of the kit: backdrop blur, refraction and tinting on
      surfaces. The default theme is solid.
    </AccordionItem>
    <AccordionItem value="a2" title="Which browsers support backdrop-filter?">
      All modern evergreen browsers. Older environments receive a fallback without the blur effect.
    </AccordionItem>
    <AccordionItem value="a3" title="Can I nest a Surface inside a Card?">
      Yes. A Card is a Surface, so any Surface can sit inside it.
    </AccordionItem>
  </Accordion>
</div>
