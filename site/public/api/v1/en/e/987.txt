Element: #987 A whole page (layout/preview-stage)
What it shows: A whole page (the sign-in template) in a PreviewStage iframe, laid out at the chosen width with its own media queries.
When to use: Showing a full page or a template at real phone, tablet and desktop widths with isolated styles.
Spec:
- `<PreviewStage label="Sign in template" src="/templates/auth/sign-in/" height={420}`
- `widths={[390, 768, 1280]}` are the presets next to Fill
- The page renders in an isolated iframe: real viewport width, real media queries, no style leakage
Tags: preview-stage, iframe, page, template, responsive
Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.5.0
Components: PreviewStage
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/layout/preview-stage#a-whole-page
