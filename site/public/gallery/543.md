# #543 Paper grain

Three solid Surface tiles: static paper grain, animated paper grain and no grain.

When to use: Giving a card or panel a printed-paper texture for editorial and marketing layouts.

## Facets

- Kind: component / surface
- Style: default, skeuo
- Motion: subtle
- Interaction: none
- Layout: grid
- Color mode: both
- Density: regular
- Tech: css (cost: css)
- Reduced motion: handled; keyboard: not interactive
- Components: Surface
- Also searched as: paper texture, noise, film grain

## Spec

- `<Surface material="solid" elevation={2} grain p="4" class="tile">` is the static grain
- `grain="animated"` shifts the noise and stays still under reduced motion
- The third tile has no `grain` prop
