# #068 Tone and glow

Two Cards side by side: one with a brand tint and one with a brand glow, each with a title and one line of text.

When to use: Highlighting a featured or selected card without changing its content layout.

## Facets

- Kind: component / surface
- Style: default
- Motion: none
- Interaction: none
- Layout: grid
- Color mode: both
- Density: regular
- Tech: css (cost: css)
- Reduced motion: handled; keyboard: not interactive
- Components: Card
- Also searched as: highlight card, featured card, halo

## Spec

- `<Card tone="brand" class="accent">` titled Tinted
- `<Card glow class="accent">` titled Glowing
- Both cards share `class="accent"` styled with `flex: 1` and `min-width: 200px`
- Card content is a `.title` paragraph and a `.text` paragraph

Needs: @codeatlasdev/ui@4.6.0
Live demo: https://ui.codeatlas.com.br/docs/surfaces/card#tone-and-glow
