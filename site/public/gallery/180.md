# #180 Container and Section

A Container and Section holding a Stack of two plan cards, showing the page-level layout primitives.

When to use: Structuring a marketing or settings page with a centered container, a labelled section and stacked cards.

## Spec

- `<Container size="md">` wraps `<Section size="1" aria-label="Plans">`
- `<Stack gap="4">` holds 2 cards with `material="solid"` and `headingLevel={3}`
- Cards are `title="Starter"` (Up to 3 projects.) and `title="Team"` (Unlimited projects and roles.)
