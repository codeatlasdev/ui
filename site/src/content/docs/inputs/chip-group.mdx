```svelte
<script>
  import { ChipGroup } from "@codeatlasdev/ui";
  let size = $state("");
</script>

<ChipGroup
  label="Sales team size"
  name="teamSize"
  bind:value={size}
  items={[{ value: "1", label: "Just me" }, { value: "2-5", label: "2 to 5" }]}
  error={errors.teamSize}
/>
```

Use it for one choice among 3 to 8 short options. [RadioGroup](/docs/inputs/radio/) is a vertical list or cards (too tall for short options) and [SegmentedControl](/docs/inputs/segmented-control/) never wraps, so it overflows a 390px screen.

The group is a real `radiogroup` of native radios. Tab enters on the selected chip (or the first one) and leaves on the next Tab; the arrows, Home and End move focus and selection; Space and Enter select. Disabled chips are skipped. Below `sm` (600px) the chips sit in three equal columns, so five short options are two rows and never exceed the container; from `sm` they take their own width and wrap freely. The selected chip has a brand outline and fill plus a check icon, so colour is never the only signal, and every chip is at least 44px tall.

`error` renders `role="alert"` under the group and sets `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` on it; `description` is announced through `aria-describedby` too. `name` makes the value part of `FormData` on a plain form submit, and `required` blocks a native submit until a chip is picked. A visible `label` names the group; with `hideLabel` or without `label`, pass `aria-label`.
