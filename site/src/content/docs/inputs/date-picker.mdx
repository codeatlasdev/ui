```svelte
<script>
  import { DatePicker, CalendarDate } from "@codeatlasdev/ui";
  let date = $state(new CalendarDate(2026, 10, 10));
</script>

<DatePicker label="Delivery date" bind:value={date} />
```

Values are `@internationalized/date` objects. `CalendarDate`, `Time`, `today`, `getLocalTimeZone` and the `DateValue`, `DateRange` and `TimeValue` types are re-exported from `@codeatlasdev/ui`, so you do not need that package as a direct dependency.

The default `locale` is `pt-BR` with Monday-first weeks. The demos below pass `locale="en-US"` and `weekStartsOn={0}` to show the English format.

**Keyboard.** In field segments, type digits or use the up and down arrows to change a value, and the left and right arrows or Tab to move between segments. In a calendar, left and right move by day, up and down by week, PageUp and PageDown by month, Shift with PageUp or PageDown by year, Home and End to the week edges, Enter or Space selects, and Escape closes the popover.

An `error` is rendered with `role="alert"` and sets `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`. Clicking a field `label` focuses its first segment.
