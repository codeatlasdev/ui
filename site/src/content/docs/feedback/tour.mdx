Each step points at one element (a CSS selector or a function returning it), scrolls it into view and rings it, and shows a small dialog named by the step's title, with Back, Next or Done, a skip button and "2 of 5". Focus moves to the next action on every step and Escape skips the tour. While a modal is open over the page the tour hides, and it comes back when the modal closes.

```svelte
<Tour bind:open steps={[
  { target: "#new-deal", title: "Create a deal", body: "Start here." },
  { target: "[data-tour=filters]", title: "Filter", body: "Narrow the board.", side: "right" },
]} onclose={(reason) => saveSeen(reason)} />
```

Keep tours short (three to five steps) and let people leave at any step.
