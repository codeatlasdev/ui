## Variants

- `boxed` (default): a bordered, rounded box. Use it on a page body or a plain background, where the section is the surface.
- `flush`: no border, radius or background, and no inline padding, so the title, chevron and body sit on the gutter of whatever contains them. Use it inside a panel, drawer or side sheet that is already a surface; the trigger keeps a `--ca-touch-target` minimum height. Add `divided` to draw a subtle top border between consecutive sections.

In every variant the trigger is transparent, and only reads `--ca-glass-bg` under the `glass` and `frosted` surface styles.
