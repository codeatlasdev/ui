The effects people ask for on a first screen: a light that follows the cursor, a surface that reacts to a click, a background that never sits still. Every one runs inside [`MotionStage`](/docs/motion/motion-stage/), so it plays only in view, stops when you touch it and shows its final state under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Ambient effects are decoration: keep them behind the content and never carry meaning in them.
