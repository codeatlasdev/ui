A transition is the motion between two states of the same screen. Each demo below wraps real package components (`Card`, `Tabs`, `Chip`, `Button`) and animates around them; none of them is forked. Every demo is wrapped in [`MotionStage`](/docs/motion/motion-stage/): it plays only in view, stops when you touch it, and shows the final state under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## How to ask for one

Say the name, or the number. `Use #360 for the plan cards` and `Use a shared element transition from the plan list to its detail` resolve to the same reference, with the exact values.
