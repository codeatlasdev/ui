Text is the cheapest place to add motion and the easiest to break: split words that a screen reader spells out, counters that announce every frame, tickers nobody can stop. Every demo here keeps the real sentence or number as the accessible name, hides the animated copy with `aria-hidden`, shows the final text under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and can be taken over by touching it. Ask an AI for one by name or by number: `Use #300` or `Use a split text reveal on the hero headline`.
