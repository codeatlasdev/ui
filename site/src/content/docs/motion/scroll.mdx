Scroll motion ties an effect to where the reader is on the page. Every demo below scrolls **inside its own container**, so it works in a card, a gallery tile or a modal, and the stage scrolls it for you while it is in view. Scroll it yourself (wheel, trackpad or touch) to take over; the Pause button stops the autoplay.

Where the browser supports **CSS scroll-driven animations** (`animation-timeline: scroll()` and `view()`), the effect runs on the compositor with no JavaScript. Where it does not, a passive `scroll` listener writes the same progress to a custom property and the same transforms read it. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the container shows its final state and does not scroll by itself.
