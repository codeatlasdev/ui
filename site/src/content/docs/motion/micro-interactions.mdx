A micro-interaction is the small reply an element gives to a touch: it leans, presses, flips, confirms. Each demo below plays itself while in view with a faint fake pointer (hidden from assistive tech), and the moment you touch it, it is yours: the real pointer or keyboard drives the same effect. Under reduced motion every demo shows its final state. Read [Motion](/docs/motion/) for the contract.
