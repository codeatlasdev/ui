`Highlight` renders a native `<mark>` painted as a marker stroke across the lower half of the line. With `animated` the marker sweeps in once on mount with `--ca-ease-out-expo`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, or `--ca-motion-scale: 0`, there is no animation and the marker is drawn complete. The color is `--ca-highlight-color` (a brand tint by default); under forced colors it uses the system `Mark` color. It wraps across lines without breaking.
