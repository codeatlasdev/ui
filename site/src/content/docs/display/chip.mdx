A chip becomes a keyboard-focusable toggle button when `onclick` is set: Enter and Space activate it and `aria-pressed` follows `selected`. `StatusChip` is a separate, non-interactive component for dot + label statuses.

## ChipRow

A chip is 28px tall and gets its 44px touch target from an invisible overhang. Any scroller clips that overhang (`overflow-x: auto` makes `overflow-y` clip too), so the taps just above and below a chip in a horizontally scrolling row miss. `ChipRow` is that row: `role="group"` with your `label`, children that never shrink, no scrollbar, `overscroll-behavior-x: contain`, and under `pointer: coarse` it pads the scroller by `(--ca-touch-target - chip height) / 2` and cancels it with a negative margin, so the hit area is whole and the layout height is the chips'. Use `size="sm"` for `sm` chips, `fade` for an edge fade, `wrap` to wrap instead of scroll. It suits any row of chip-sized controls, `ToggleGroup` items included.
