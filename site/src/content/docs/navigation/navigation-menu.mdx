```svelte
</script>

<NavigationMenu aria-label="Main">
  <NavigationMenuItem label="Products">
    <NavigationMenuLink href="/analytics" description="Dashboards">Analytics</NavigationMenuLink>
    <NavigationMenuLink href="/automation">Automation</NavigationMenuLink>
  </NavigationMenuItem>
  <NavigationMenuLink href="/pricing" current>Pricing</NavigationMenuLink>
</NavigationMenu>
```

`NavigationMenu` is the site navigation of a marketing header: a `<nav>` with a list of plain links and disclosure buttons that reveal a panel of links. It follows the WAI-ARIA disclosure navigation pattern, not the menu pattern: there is no `role="menu"`, `menubar` or `menuitem`, links stay links, and a screen reader reads the list, the buttons with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and the links in the panel with their normal Tab order. For application commands (File, Edit) use [Menubar](/docs/navigation/menubar/); for actions on one button use [DropdownMenu](/docs/navigation/menu/).

The top level is one tab stop with arrow keys: Left and Right (and Home and End) move between the buttons and links without opening anything. Enter or Space toggles a panel and focus stays on the button; Tab then moves into the panel's links and on to the next top-level item; Down opens the panel and moves to its first link; Up and Down move through the links; Escape closes the panel and returns focus to its button. Following a link closes the panel, and so does a press outside. Only one panel is open at a time.

With a pointer, resting on a button for `openDelay` (150 ms) opens its panel, moving to another button while one is open switches at once, and leaving closes after `closeDelay` (250 ms) so the pointer can cross into the panel. A click toggles the panel, which is also how touch opens it. A `description` on a panel link adds a second line that is part of the link's accessible name. Mark the current page with `current` (`aria-current="page"`). Build a mobile version of the header with [Drawer](/docs/feedback/drawer/); this component is the desktop bar.
