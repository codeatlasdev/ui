CodeAtlas UI gives Svelte 5 apps full screens, not just bricks: page templates, an app shell, data tables, charts, forms and overlays, all built on a headless layer of our own and themed through CSS custom properties.

```svelte
<AppList title="Users" data={users} searchKeys={["name", "email"]}>
  {#snippet children(list)}
    <SimpleTable>
      {#each list.rows as u (u.id)}
        <tr><td><UserCell name={u.name} email={u.email} /></td></tr>
      {/each}
    </SimpleTable>
  {/snippet}
</AppList>
```

That is a whole users page: header, search, pagination, empty state and glass surface, with no CSS written.

## What you get

- **Pages, not primitives.** `AppList`, `AppForm`, `AppSettings`, `PageShell` and `AppShellLayout` bring layout, spacing and empty states.
- **Liquid Glass.** Five material tiers with real `backdrop-filter`, tuned for light and dark, with opaque fallbacks for reduced transparency.
- **Theme engine.** Eight OKLCH presets, brand presets from one hex color, four surface styles, and no flash on load.
- **Native on mobile.** Safe areas, keyboard handling, 44px touch targets, bottom sheets with swipe to dismiss, and browser chrome color that follows the page.
- **Accessible.** WAI-ARIA keyboard and focus behavior, axe-tested components, reduced motion and reduced transparency support.
- **Light.** Charts, DataTable and CodeSnippet are separate entry points with optional peers, so they stay out of your bundle unless imported.
- **Built for AI agents.** Closed prop vocabularies, typed props, `llms.txt` and installable skills.
