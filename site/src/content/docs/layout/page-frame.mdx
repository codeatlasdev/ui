Use `PageFrame` when a live stage (a map, a canvas) sits behind the page content. `PageShell` and `AppList` own the whole content area; `PageFrame` lays the page out *over* the stage and tells the stage what it covers. Place it as the page content of `AppShellLayout`: it fills the content area and lays its panel out inside it, leaving the rail and panel alone.

| `mode` | Desktop | Below 900px |
|---|---|---|
| `map` | A floating panel (`side`, `width`). The stage around it stays interactive. | Full screen, or with `sheet` a non-modal bottom sheet. |
| `split` | A work area of about 60% (tables, kanban) plus a stage strip. | Full screen; `mobileStage` shows the stage instead. |
| `page` | An opaque full page. The stage is hidden (`data-ca-stage="hidden"` on `<html>`). | The same. |

`header`, `toolbar` and `footer` stay fixed; only the body scrolls (`overscroll-behavior: contain`). The body is keyboard-scrollable while it overflows.

## Covered area

The frame publishes the rectangle it covers, in px from each edge of the stage, on `stageInsets`. It updates on resize, `mode`, `side`, `width` and every sheet height, and resets to zero when the frame unmounts. When two frames are mounted the newest one wins and the previous one takes over when it leaves.

```ts
import { stageInsets } from "@codeatlasdev/ui";

stageInsets.current; // { top, right, bottom, left }, reactive
map.fitBounds(bounds, { padding: stageInsets.current });
```

Without JavaScript, read `--ca-stage-inset-top`, `-right`, `-bottom` and `-left` on `<html>`. Never copy the panel widths or the 45% sheet height into the app: they are tokens of the frame.

## Bottom sheet

With `sheet`, `mode="map"` below 900px is a bottom sheet, not a `Sheet`: it is **not modal** (no backdrop, no focus trap, no scroll lock, no `inert`), so the stage above it still takes taps and pans. Its height snaps to a `detent`: `peek` (header only), `half` (45%, the default) or `full`. Drag the handle, or focus it and press Arrow Up and Arrow Down (Home and End jump to the ends). `bind:detent` follows the user.
